# Roadmap

What is planned, roughly in order. Nothing here has a date.

## Now: talking with the DJ

- **All DJ settings.** Every rule the DJ follows can be changed: when a skip counts against a song, when a song counts as
  disliked, how long it remembers, how soon a song may come back, how many songs per artist, how often it plays a
  wildcard. The common ones in the main list, the math under "Advanced".
- **DJ state page.** The songs the DJ weighed for its last pick, each with its score and what the score is made of
  (fits this sitting, long-term taste, similar to the song playing, new, played lately, disliked).
- **"Why this song?"** A short reason under each song the DJ picked: "close to the shoegaze you just heard", "not
  played for 3 months", "new in the library".
- **"More like this" / "Less like this".** A clearer signal than a skip. Every such choice is listed in the settings
  and can be taken back.
- **Undo a skip.** When a skip counts against a song, a small notice offers to take it back, for the times you skipped
  because of the moment, not the song.

## Next

- **Steer the DJ.** Buttons for "calmer", "livelier" and "songs I don't know", from the BPM and mood tags the DJ
  already reads.
- **Taste by time of day.** Mornings and nights can sound different: the DJ learns what you play at each hour.
- **"More / less like this" with Navidrome stars.** Once the DJ's own list has proven itself, tie it to the server's
  stars and ratings, so the choice follows you to other devices.

## Known gaps

- Seeking to the end of a song counts as hearing it in full (needs a played-time counter).
- When resetting the DJ memory fails both to delete and to write the file, the app does not say so.
